Spanish Lesson 1: Greetings & Introductions

Learn to greet others with proper native pronunciation.

Grammar Focus: Formal vs. Informal

In Spanish, it is very important to know when to use the formal "you" (usted) versus the informal "you" (tú).

  • Use tú with friends, family, and people your own age. (e.g., ¿Cómo estás (tú)?)
  • Use usted with elders, strangers, or in professional settings to show respect. (e.g., ¿Cómo está (usted)?)

Note: The inverted question mark (¿) is always placed at the beginning of an interrogative sentence. Also, personal pronouns (I, you) are often omitted because the verb ending already tells us who the subject is.

Practice Dialogue

Read the following conversation to see how these greetings are used in context.

Maria: ¡Hola, Carlos! ¿Cómo estás?

Carlos: Hola Maria. Muy bien, gracias. ¿Y tú?

Maria: Bien, gracias. ¡Hasta luego!

Carlos: ¡Hasta luego!
